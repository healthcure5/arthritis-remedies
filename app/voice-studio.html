Hinge App

Voice studio

This page records every line the Hinge App speaks, using Kokoro, a free, natural-sounding voice that is licensed for commercial use. It all runs in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Before you start

Use a computer with Chrome or Edge if you can. It works on a phone too, but it's much slower. The first time, the browser downloads the voice (about 90 MB). Then recording takes roughly 10 to 25 minutes. Keep this tab open until it finishes.
