About Hinge Care

Plain-spoken, sourced help for people with arthritis and the families who care about them.

What Hinge Care is

Hinge Care is a small team that writes plain-spoken help for people living with arthritis and for the grown children who worry about them. We read what the NIH, the CDC, the Mayo Clinic and the Arthritis Foundation say, and put it in words you can use at the kitchen table. Every journal page links its sources.

  • Easier Mornings: a free five-day email challenge, one small thing to try each morning.
