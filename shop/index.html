Who it's for

Adults 55 and older whose joints feel stiff or sore, and who would like calm, simple routines they can do at home, at their own pace.

Grown children who've noticed Mom holding the railing a little tighter, and want to help without making a big deal of it.

Our safety guidelines

  • Every move has a seated version.
  • Move slowly. A gentle pull is fine. Sharp or worsening pain means stop.
  • If you have a hot, red, very swollen joint, a recent fall or recent surgery, please check with your doctor first.
