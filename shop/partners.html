How to share it well

Please do

  • Share why you like the routines, recipes or community, in your own words.
  • Say that it is for general wellness and is not medical advice.
  • Say that you earn a commission, for example: "I earn a share if you join."
  • Tell people they can cancel any time.
  • Mention that every move has a seated version.

Please don't

  • Say it cures, treats or reverses arthritis or any condition.
  • Promise pain relief or a certain result.
  • Use countdown timers or fake urgency.
  • Share a member story without their written permission.
