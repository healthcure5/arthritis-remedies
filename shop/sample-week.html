Morning Stiffness

This week is about the first hour of the day, when joints often feel tightest and getting going takes the most out of you. Every part is gentle and short, and you can do it seated.

Monday

Your gentle wake-up routine

About ten minutes. It starts in bed and finishes in a chair. Go slowly.

  1. Settle in. Lie on your back, or sit on the edge of the bed. Take three slow breaths.
  2. Ankle pumps. Point your toes away, then pull them back toward you. Slow and steady.
  3. Finger stretches. Make a soft fist, then open your hand wide. A loose fist is enough.
  4. Knee rocks. Knees bent, feet flat, let both knees drift a little to one side, then the other. Seated: slide one heel forward along the floor, then back.
  5. Wrist circles. Elbows by your sides, small circles, then change direction.
  6. Move to a chair. Take your time getting up. Sit near the front of a sturdy chair.
  7. Shoulder rolls. Up, back and down, big slow circles.
  8. Seated side reach. One hand on the chair, raise the other arm and lean gently away.
  9. Seated marching. Hold the sides of the chair and lift one knee a few inches, then the other.
  10. Close. Hands on your lap, three slow breaths. That's your morning done.

Wednesday

Warm oat and berry bowl

About ten minutes, one pot, easy to stir. Serves one.

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats and 1 cup milk (or water) in a small pot over medium heat, stirred for about 5 minutes
  • Stir in half of 1/2 cup blueberries
  • Top with the rest of the berries, 1 tablespoon chopped walnuts, 1 teaspoon ground flaxseed and a pinch of cinnamon

Easier on the hands: buy walnuts already chopped and use frozen berries.

Friday

This weekend's small win: three easy mornings

Saturday, Sunday and Monday, try three small things:

  • Before you get up: the first five moves of Monday's routine, in bed.
  • Within an hour of waking: something warm to drink.
  • Before lunch: write one word for how your joints feel.

If you only manage one part on one day, that still counts.
