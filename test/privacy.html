Your privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

You trusted us with your details, so here is exactly what we do with them, in plain words.

What we collect

When you ask for our free guide, we collect only what you type into the form:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to give it
  • Your agreement to receive joint-care tips by email

We do not ask for health records, payment details or anything else.

How we use it

  • To email your guide to the address you gave us
  • To send joint-care tips by email, which you agree to when you ask for the guide
  • To reply if you contact us

We never sell your details, and we never pass them to advertisers.

Services that help us run this site

A few outside services handle parts of this site, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • FormSubmit receives the form, passes your details to us by email and sends your guide. It uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam.
  • Our payment partner handles payment on its own secure page if you choose to buy The Joint Reset. We never see or store your card details.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, may record technical details such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. Your browser briefly remembers the email address you typed, only so the thank-you page can show it back to you. That stays on your own device and clears when you close the tab.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to send your guide and your tips. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask to see the details we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete them
  • Stop the tips emails at any time

Use the form below and we will take care of it.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect details from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your details, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions, or want your details removed? Send us a note.
