Your 30 days with us

Here's what we do, and when. You're never left wondering what comes next.

  1. 1

    Day 1: you tell us about your joints

    A short set of questions on your phone or computer: which joints hurt, how your day goes, what you eat, what you've tried, and what you want to get back to. A son or daughter can help you fill it in.

  2. 2

    Days 2 and 3: we build your plan

    We read every answer and write your plan around your day and the one thing you want back. Your My Plan page opens, your morning emails begin, and your large-print plan arrives.

  3. 3

    Week 1: your first check-in

    We ask how the first week went. Reply in a few lines, and we rewrite your plan to fit what you told us.

  4. 4

    Week 2: we check in again

    What helped, what didn't, what felt like too much. You tell us, and your next plan reflects it.

  5. 5

    Week 4: your last check-in and your report

    Your pain number on day 1 next to day 30, and a fourth plan to keep going after your 30 days are up.

  6. ✉

    Any day in between: write to us

    Any questions? Send them to arthritis@myhingecare.com, and we'll write back to you.

What people ask

Do I need the Program first?

No. Your plan works on its own. If you have the Program, your plan tells you which lessons to open first, so the two work well together.

How soon does my plan start?

Within 3 days of sending your answers. Your My Plan page opens, your morning emails begin, and your plan arrives by email from arthritis@myhingecare.com.

Can I buy it for my mom or dad?

Yes. Buy it, then fill in the questions together or send them the link. The plan is written for them, in their name.

What if the plan doesn't feel right?

Tell us at any check-in, and we change it. If it still isn't right for you, ask for a full refund within 30 days.

Why do you ask about my medicines?

So we never suggest a herb, rub or food that doesn't mix well with what you take. Keep taking what your doctor prescribed. Your answers go only to our private inbox and are never shared or sold.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can open an email, you can follow your plan. Each morning's email has a big button to today's steps, and your large-print plan works on paper too.

Will I have to get on a call?

No. Your access to the team is all in writing. Write to us when it suits you, and read our answer when you're ready.
