Why we started it

Our book and our Program tell you what helps. They can't notice when you've gone quiet for a week.

That's usually when things slip. A bad night turns into a lazy week, the walks stop, the stiffness creeps back and you start to think nothing works for you.

The Circle is there for that week. Someone asks how your goal went. Somebody else says they had the same kind of Tuesday. You get back to it a little sooner than you would have alone.

How a week goes

One call, one check-in, one small goal

Tuesday at 7pm Eastern we meet on video. The first 20 minutes are gentle movement you can do in a chair or standing, at your own pace. Then it's your questions about pacing, flares, sleep, food and sticking with it. You leave with one small goal for the week. If you miss a call, the recording is waiting for you the next day.

Midweek you get a short check-in by text or email. How's the goal going, and what got in the way? A two-line answer is plenty.

Any day you can post in the private member group. Share the small wins there, and the bad days too.

Is it for you?

Who The Circle fits

  • You have stiff, sore joints from arthritis, and you'd like to move more without making things worse.
  • You've tried doing it alone and keep losing steam after a few weeks.
  • You can make most Tuesday evenings, or watch the recording soon after.
  • You're happy to keep your doctor in charge of your medical care.

We're not doctors, and The Circle isn't medical care. We won't diagnose anything or tell you what to do about your medicine. If a joint is suddenly hot, red and very painful, especially with a fever, please get care the same day.

Questions people ask

Before you apply

Do I need to be fit or flexible?

No. Every move has a seated version, and you go only as far as feels okay that day.

What do I need for the calls?

A phone, tablet or computer with internet. We email you the video link each week. You can keep your camera off if you'd rather.

What if I miss a Tuesday?

Watch the recording the next day, and answer the midweek check-in as usual.

How do I pay?

Nothing today. If we both agree on the call that The Circle fits you, we send you a secure payment link.

Why is there an application?

The group is small, so we want everyone in it to be a good fit. The call also lets us point you somewhere better if what you need is a doctor.
