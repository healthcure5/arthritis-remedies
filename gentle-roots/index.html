Sound familiar?

It doesn't happen in one big fight

It happens over a few hundred ordinary evenings. One of you scrolls, the other does the dishes. "Did you pay the water bill?" is the longest talk of the day. You go to bed at different times. Nobody did anything wrong, and somehow you're further apart than you've ever been.

Most couples never tell anyone they feel this way. They figure it's just what happens after a few years. But distance like this is built from habits, and the same small moments that built it can bring you back.

That's what everything here is for: small, warm things you do together at home, on an ordinary night, that bring back the feeling of being on the same side. No homework, and no weekend retreat you can't afford.
