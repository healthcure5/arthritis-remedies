Why questions work

Couples who feel distant often still talk every day. They just talk about the same things: plans, chores, kids, money. A good question breaks the loop. It reminds you that the person across from you still has a whole inner world you get to visit.

The cards start light and get a little deeper as you go, so nobody feels put on the spot.
